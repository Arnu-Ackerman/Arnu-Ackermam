A little about me

Learning should feel encouraging, not intimidating.

Hi, I'm Arnu — your friendly English tutor from South Africa. I'm patient, approachable and passionate about helping students express themselves clearly and naturally in English.

My background includes teaching, training colleagues, sales and helping customers understand their options. These experiences taught me how to listen, explain ideas clearly and adapt to different people and needs.

I love music, travelling and meeting people from different cultures. I believe mistakes are a natural and important part of learning: we'll use them as opportunities to improve, without pressure or embarrassment.
